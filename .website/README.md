## Glitter & Gunfire

**ALERT!** Alien lifeforms detected.

A sinister force has taken hold in the depths of the moon base.  Personnel in the area have reported rainbows and screams coming from the subterranean vault. Take the elevator in cargo bay and neutralize the threat. I know you have only been issued a sidearm, but try to find some more weapons en route. I have a feeling you'll need some serious firepower.
